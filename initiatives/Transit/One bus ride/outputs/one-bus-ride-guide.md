# One bus ride — MetroRapid 801 things to do

A single CapMetro ride with a short walk-and-see loop. Public places only. Check [CapMetro](https://www.capmetro.org/) for today’s 801 times before you go.

## The ride

Board MetroRapid **801** near downtown on the North Lamar / South Congress spine. Ride south toward South Congress. Stay on the bus for a few stops so the corridor makes sense as one line, not a puzzle of transfers.

## Three public beats

1. **Capitol edge** — Start close to the Texas State Capitol grounds. Orient yourself on the north–south spine before you board.
2. **South Congress** — Alight when you reach the South Congress strip. Walk a few blocks for food, shops, and street life. Everything here is public-facing retail and sidewalk.
3. **Corridor context** — 801 also ties toward the Domain area on the north end of the spine. You do not need that end for this loop; knowing it exists is enough to feel the line’s shape.

## Loop and done

After South Congress, either reverse on 801 back downtown or end the outing there. Keep it to one ride and one neighborhood walk. No app install, no ticket hack, no private guest lists.

## Schedule caveat

Headways change. This page does not invent clock times. Open CapMetro’s public 801 schedule the morning you ride and trust that over any printed guess.
