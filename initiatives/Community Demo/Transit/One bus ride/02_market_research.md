# Research — One bus ride

**Status:** Approved (demo)

## Landscape (public sources)

- CapMetro publishes MetroRapid 801 as a frequent north–south spine (North Lamar ↔ South Congress). Schedules and maps are public on capmetro.org.
- Public places along or near the corridor: Texas State Capitol area, Guadalupe / UT edge, South Congress shops and food, Domain area toward the north end of the corridor.

## Data sufficiency decision (R-D02)

**PASS — Food fallback not triggered.** Public CapMetro 801 materials plus public points of interest are enough for a one-page things-to-do guide. No partner APIs, no private lists, no cowork-repo content required.

## Synthesis

Build a single outbound-style ride: board near downtown / Capitol, ride south on 801 toward South Congress, note two public stops, alight for a short walk-and-see loop, reverse or end. Keep times as "check CapMetro for today’s headways" rather than inventing clock times that go stale.

## Decision

Proceed to PRD for a bounded guide artifact.
